Account & Data Deletion

SVIS — Songdee Vehicle Inspection System (com.songdeedev.svis)

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SVIS account and the personal data associated with it. The app has no self-serve account signup — accounts are created by your fleet organisation — so deletion requests are handled by us on your behalf.

How to request deletion

Email us with the subject line “SVIS – Account deletion request” and include your company, username, and assigned fleet so we can verify and locate your account:

Request account deletion by email

You may also ask your fleet administrator to submit the request on your behalf.

What gets deleted

  • Account data: username, hashed password, first/last name, role, and fleet assignment are permanently deleted.
  • Photos you uploaded to evidence defects or repairs are permanently deleted.
  • Inspection records (vehicle ID, date, pass/fail, notes) tied to you are either deleted or disassociated from your identity (anonymised).

What may be retained, and why

The fleet organisation that operates the vehicles may need to keep certain inspection and repair records as operational or legal safety records. Where retained, these are kept in anonymised form (no longer linked to you) and are purged under our standard retention window — inspection logs and issue reports are automatically deleted after 6 months.

Timeline

We confirm receipt within a few business days and complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request.
