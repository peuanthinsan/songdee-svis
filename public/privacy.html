Privacy Policy

SVIS — Songdee Vehicle Inspection System

Last updated: July 27, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: username, hashed password, first/last name, company, role, and assigned fleet.
  • Inspection data: vehicle ID, date, pass/fail per checklist item, free-text notes.
  • Photos you take in the app to evidence defects or repairs. Photos are uploaded to our storage and accessible via signed links.
  • Network/device basics required to operate the app (e.g. crash diagnostics, time stamps).

What we don't collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone, calendar, advertising identifiers, or browsing history.

How we use it

To record fleet inspections, surface failed checks to fleet managers via email, track repair status, and produce historical reports for the customer organisation that licenses this app.

Sharing

Each customer company's data is logically isolated. Data is shared only with: the company operating the fleet, infrastructure providers (Vercel, Neon, SendGrid) acting as data processors, and authorities when legally required.

Retention

Inspection logs and issue reports are retained for 6 months by default, then purged by an automatic cleanup job. Account data is retained while the account is active.

Your rights

You can request export or deletion of your data by contacting your fleet administrator, or emailing peuan@songdeegps.com. To delete your account and associated data, see Account & Data Deletion.
